🕵️ What is Imposter?

A social guessing game. Everyone knows the same secret word, except one person: the imposter. The imposter must blend in and avoid getting caught.

How to play

  • Enter the number of players and every player’s name.
  • Pass the phone around. Each player secretly checks their word.
  • The imposter sees “YOU ARE THE IMPOSTER” instead of the word.
  • Take turns giving one short English clue about the word.
  • Never say the word itself!
  • When time is up (or when you decide), everyone votes in turn on the same phone.

Winning

  • The group wins if the imposter gets the most votes on their own.
  • The imposter wins if someone else gets the most votes, or if there is a tie.

Tips

  • Imposter: listen carefully and act like you know.
  • Others: be clear enough for your team but vague enough to fool the imposter.
